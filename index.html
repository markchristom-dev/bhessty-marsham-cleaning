Questions

Frequently asked questions

How do I book?

Use the price calculator above and press "Send request on WhatsApp", or message us directly on +971 52 453 6811. We confirm your slot and price in the chat.

What does "Service hours 24/7" mean?

You can send us a request at any time of day, any day. We'll reply on WhatsApp to confirm an available time slot for your clean.

What's the difference between standard and deep cleaning?

Standard cleaning (35 AED/hr) covers regular upkeep. Deep cleaning (40 AED/hr) is a more thorough clean and includes vacuuming and window cleaning.

How many hours do I need?

The calculator suggests hours based on your property type. Every home is different, so adjust the hours or ask us on WhatsApp for advice.

Is the price on the calculator final?

It's an estimate. We confirm the final price with you on WhatsApp before the job.

When do I pay?

After the job is finished.

Can you supply amenities for my holiday home?

Yes. Vanity kits, towel sets (8 pcs), facial tissue, toilet paper, shampoo, conditioner and shower gel can be added to your booking. Prices are shown in the calculator.

Do you clean offices?

Yes. Choose "Office" in the calculator or message us with your office size and preferred schedule.
